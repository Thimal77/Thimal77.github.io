01 · About

Engineering solutions that
move the real world forward

I'm a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Peradeniya, driven by one belief — technology is most powerful when it solves problems people actually live with. From building real-time IoT systems that monitor crops in the field, to designing emergency response networks that connect first responders within seconds, my work sits at the intersection of software, hardware, and human impact. I bring hands-on experience across full-stack development, embedded systems, cloud infrastructure, and machine learning to every project I take on.

Beyond the code, I'm fascinated by the bigger picture — how intelligent systems can predict failures before they happen, how edge computing can bring AI to places with no reliable internet, and how well-designed software can quietly transform how communities work, travel, and stay safe. I thrive in collaborative, fast-moving environments where curiosity is a skill and shipping something meaningful is the goal. Currently seeking opportunities where engineering depth meets real-world ambition.
